# Betting at T-60 or 11:00 on the corrected forecast: closing-line value (research 105, 15 November 2026)

Question: research 104 found the price an hour before the off makes a
stronger forecast than the 11:00 price. Does betting at that price on the
corrected forecast's disagreements beat Betfair SP?

`research/t60_clv_105/run.py`. Forecast: the native 87-input forecast plus
the joint correction with the price group (`with-3600` or `with-1100`),
normalised within the race. Scored quarters 2025Q3–2026Q2. Retrospective:
the recipe and variants were chosen on these quarters; hold-out untouched.

- Taken price: last traded price at the cut, reduced for runners withdrawn
  after it (`rf_after_*`), capped at 51.
- Selection: `value_bet_selection` against the race-normalised share of the
  field at the cut.
- Settlement: exchange commission 2%; ROI interval resamples races.
- CLV: taken / BSP, geometric mean and median.

+2.3% to +4.6%; 11:00 +3.2% to +11.1%). By odds, ROI rises at long prices
(11:00, 25–51: +23%, geo CLV 1.074), where variance is largest.

## Reading

- The corrected forecast with a morning price beats the close
  retrospectively, at both cuts and in every quarter.
- **For betting, 11:00 is better than T-60**, the opposite of the accuracy
  ranking. An earlier price leaves more of the day's money still to come,
  and the model leans the way that money moves.
- T-60's edge is about one tick: it disappears at one tick worse on all
  bets. The 11:00 edge survives two ticks at threshold 0.02.
- Not settled:
  - LTP is not a guaranteed fill, and 11:00 markets are thin, with wide
    spreads at long prices, where the ROI concentrates.
  - Recipe and variant were chosen on these quarters.

  A pre-registered single look at the hold-out, and live best-back prices
  (a forward paper record), are what would settle it.

## Does the corrected forecast add anything to BSP?

A two-weight blend, `a·log p_BSP + b·log p_model`, renormalised within the
race, fitted on earlier quarters and scored on the next (2025Q4–2026Q2,
8,232 races):

| Model in the blend | Weights (BSP, model) | Against BSP alone (1.74018) |
|---|---|---:|
| Native 87-input forecast | 0.95, 0.04–0.05 | −0.00009 |
| Corrected forecast (`more-groups-deeper`) | 0.94, 0.05–0.07 | −0.00044 [−0.00091, +0.00006], 3/3 |

The correction adds a sliver beyond the close: right sign in every quarter,
5x the native model's gain, still not significant and far too small to
clear commission. The 11:00 result above is anticipation of where the price
goes, not information the closing price lacks.
