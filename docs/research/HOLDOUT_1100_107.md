# Pre-registration: the 11:00 betting rule on the hold-out (research 107)

Registered 15 November 2026, before any hold-out race was scored by this
rule. Code: `research/holdout_1100_107/run.py`; code hash
`4d3938b7eea937bfd376c03968d49e54128769f0b5036572eed527e3375e188a` (`score`
refuses to run if any file in `CODE` has changed, or if a receipt exists).

## Why

Research 105 found, on development quarters 2025Q3–2026Q2 (examined many
times), that backing the corrected forecast's disagreements at the 11:00
exchange price beats Betfair SP: threshold 0.02, one tick worse than last
traded, geo CLV 1.026, ROI +5.4% [+1.2, +10.1]. The rule, threshold and
price convention were chosen there. This is the single test on races those
choices have not seen.

## Hold-out

Races from `config.GOLDEN_HOLDOUT_START` (11 June 2026) to the last date with
an 11:00 Betfair price and a result (Betfair archive to 31 October 2026).
The hold-out has been read before for other questions (the 10 November
corrections, the jumps margin test), never for this rule or any 11:00 price
result.

## Forecast

- Base: the stored 87-input win forecast
  (`data/processed/forecast_history`). Hold-out races come from run
  `20261114_050315_923313`, trained to 3 June 2026.
- Correction: `src.correction`, production groups (horse, market gap,
  residual, wave 2, tracking, horse state, recent entity form, franking,
  hidden form, past market moves), plus the eight 11:00 price inputs of
  `research/error_anatomy/morning_price.py`. Races off at or before 11:00
  have those inputs blanked.
- One correction fitted on every race with a forecast before 11 June 2026,
  applied unchanged to the whole hold-out. No refit inside it.

## Bets

- Selection: `src.bet_settlement.value_bet_selection`, base threshold 0.02,
  against the race-normalised 11:00 share of the field.
- Races: whole field priced at 11:00 and at BSP, off after 11:00.
- Taken price: the 11:00 last traded price, cut by the reduction factors of
  later withdrawals, then one Betfair tick worse. Above 1 and at most 51.

## Decision

**Primary:** geometric-mean CLV (taken price / BSP) of those bets. **Pass**
if its one-sided 95% lower bound, from 4,000 bootstrap draws over race days
(seed 0), is above 1.0. Nothing else decides it.

**Reported, not decisive:**
- ROI after 2% commission, races resampled;
- the same at the last traded price, and at threshold 0;
- every priced runner in the band (the same-universe baseline);
- race log loss of the corrected forecast against the stored forecast.
